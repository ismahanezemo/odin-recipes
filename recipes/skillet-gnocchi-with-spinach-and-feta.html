skillet gnocchi with spinach and feta

Description

These skillet gnocchi with spinach and feta are comforting dish that's ready in less than 30 minutes . Golden on the outside and tender inside , the pen-fried gnocchi are tossed with spinach ,feta , sun-dried tomatoes and other flavor-packed ingredients for an easy Mediterranean-inspired meal

Ingredients

  • 16 ounces package gnocchi
  • 2 tabelspoons unsalted butter
  • 1 tabelspoon sun-dried tomamto oil
  • 1/2 cup white wine , or reserved pasta water
  • 1/4 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
  • 2 tabelspoons sliced kalamata olives
  • 1 teaspoon granulated garlic
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 4 cups fresh baby spanich , roughly chopped
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese , sheep's milk if available

Steps

  1. Step 1

    2. In a large pot of salted water to a boil . Add gnocchi and cook according to pachage directions , typpically 2 minutes

    Remove with a slotted spoon to a paper towel to drain

  2. Step 2

    3. Melt butter and sun-dried tomato oil in a skillet over medium heat.

    Add gnocchi and fry for 3 and 5 minutes , turning regularly

    Add wine or reserved pasta water ,sun-dried tomatoes , olives , garlic granules , salt and pepper and cook for 2 additional minutes

  3. Step 3

    4. Stir in spinach and cook until it wilts , 1 to 2 minutes .Gently fold in feta cheese and serve.
