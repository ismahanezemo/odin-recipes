unsoggy salad

Descriptio

This salad is never soggy! Packed with quinoa , corn, white beans ,fresh tomatoes and bell pepper ,it holds up beautifully at barbecues ,picmics and potlucks

Ingredients

  • 1 lime , juiced
  • 2 tabelespoons olive oil
  • 1 tabelespoon honey
  • 1/4 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • salt and ground black pepper
  • 2 cups coocked quinoa
  • 1 can drained sweet corn
  • 1 pint cherry tomamtoes ,halved
  • 1 copped red pepper
  • 1/3 thinly sliced red onion
  • 1 rinsed and drained can chickpeas
  • 6 ounces crumbled feta
  • 1 avocado , cubed
  • chopped fresh basil

Steps

  1. step 1

    2. For dressing , whisk lime juice ,olive oil, honey ,and Dijon mustard together in a large bowl
